Placement Culture at IIIT Nagpur

IIIT Nagpur boasts a robust placement culture, emphasizing holistic development, strong industry collaboration, and tailored training programs. Our commitment to nurturing future leaders and impressive placement records make us a prime choice for aspiring professionals.

Placements - B.tech Batch (2020-2024)
  • Four students from the 2024 batch have secured placements at SMS Data Tech, Japan.
  • The highest package for the 2024 batch stands at 47 LPA.
  • The second highest package secured by our student is 45 LPA.
  • The third highest package is 44.92 LPA.
  • Eighteen students from the 2024 batch have received packages above 20 LPA.
  • A total of 154 companies visited the campus for placements of the 2024 batch.
  • The highest package for the 2024 batch is an impressive 90 LPA.
  • The average package for the 2024 batch stands at 14 LPA.
  • For Computer Science Engineering (CSE) students, the average package is 13.89 LPA, with a median package of 12 LPA.
  • For Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE) students, the average package is 14.87 LPA, with a median package of 11 LPA.
