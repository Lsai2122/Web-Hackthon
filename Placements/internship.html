Internships|

Objective :

This program aims to provide an educational and professional research experience to aspiring Engineers. To offer an opportunity to B. Tech / B.E. / MSc. (Computer Science/Electronics & Communication/Information Technology/Mathematics/Statistics) / MCA students from Institutions or Universities nationwide to enhance their exposure to the research environment at IIIT Nagpur.

Details of the Internship Program :

  1. The internship program is designed to impart knowledge and in-depth learning in the candidate's area of interest under the guidance of expert faculty.
  2. The candidate is required to determine the duration of the internship.
  3. Upon joining, each candidate must submit the following: (i) Original bonafide certificate from the candidate's currently affiliated organization, duly authorized by HoD / Principal / Supervisor on the letterhead. (ii) Resume (iii) Passport size photo (iv) Payment Receipt along with Registration Form
  4. Selected candidates must adhere to the rules and regulations of IIIT-N.
  5. Submission of a Project Report is mandatory for the award of the completion certificate.
  6. The candidate needs to demonstrate technical skills and professional ethics. h. All copyrights will be associated with IIIT Nagpur.

At IIIT Nagpur, we offer this program in the following two modes :

  1. In-campus Internship Program:
    • In this program, students are required to be physically present on the institute premises and must undertake the given assignments and projects under the supervision of an expert.
    • Necessary training and guidance on the selected projects will be provided.
    • Access to required computational and library resources will be granted.
  2. Online Internship Program :
    • In this program, students do not need to be physically present on the institute premises and must undertake the given assignments and projects under the supervision of an expert through online mode.
    • Necessary training and guidance on the selected projects will be provided.

How to Apply?

Students should directly apply to the faculty member via email by sending their latest Resume or CV (indicating grades and technical skills), with the subject line "Request for Six/Two Months Internship at IIIT Nagpur."


Students can choose the area/subject of the internship and the faculty supervisor from the following:

After obtaining approval from the faculty, students must submit the internship joining application form, along with the registration form (in the specified format) and the fee payment details to the Internship Cell and admin office via email at oaaccounts@iiitn.ac.in and internships@iiitn.ac.in.


Registration Fee Payment: The registration fee should only be paid after receiving selection confirmation from the supervisor.

Selection Process :

  • The respective faculty may conduct a telephonic or web-based interview for student selection.
  • Faculty members offering internships are solely responsible for selecting students after assessing their grades and technical skills.
  • Applying for the internship does not guarantee selection.

Eligibility for Internship:

Students pursuing B. Tech/B. E./M. Tech/M.Sc. in Computer Science, Electronics & Communication Engineering, or Information Technology are eligible. *For undergraduate students, they must have cleared the first two semesters of B.E./B. Tech.

Duration of Internship :

The internship duration will be either 2 months or 6 months. If necessary, the 2-month internship can be extended to 6 months with the supervisor's recommendation and approval from the competent authority. In such cases, the student must pay the difference amount for the extension.
