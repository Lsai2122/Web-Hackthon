Holistic Student Development at IIIT Nagpur
IIT Nagpur is committed to fostering the growth of its students in various areas of interest. Students from the CSE and ECE departments are actively encouraged to participate in a wide range of activities, including technical events, workshops, community outreach programs, sports, Swachh Bharat initiatives, entrepreneurship, internships, and more
The key activities at IIIT Nagpur include:
Institute Gathering -IG
Kshitij 2025 is IIIT Nagpur's annual sports meet, known as the Institute Gathering (IG), which aims to showcase the athletic abilities and talents of students in sports. With full zeal and enthusiasm, students from the CSE and ECE departments actively participate in various sports activities. The event fosters a spirit of sportsmanship and camaraderie, providing an opportunity for students to engage in healthy competition and enjoy the thrill of sports. More Details : Kshitij
The sports activities include:
  • Football
  • Volleyball
  • Basketball
  • Table Tennis
  • Chess
  • Badminton
  • Shotput
  • Tug-of-War
